What Are The Ingredients In GLPro?

GLPro is formulated with 8 named ingredients — including Berberine, Chromium, Cinnamon Extract, Alpha-Lipoic Acid, Bitter Melon — each listed individually below with the role it plays in the formula, so you can see exactly what you are taking before you buy.

1

Berberine

A plant alkaloid that has been studied for its potential to support healthy blood sugar levels and insulin sensitivity.

2

Chromium

An essential trace mineral that aids in the metabolism of carbohydrates and helps maintain normal blood glucose levels.

3

Cinnamon Extract

Derived from cinnamon bark, this extract is traditionally used to support healthy glucose metabolism and antioxidant activity.

4

Alpha-Lipoic Acid

A naturally occurring compound that acts as an antioxidant and may help support healthy blood sugar metabolism.

5

Bitter Melon

A tropical plant extract that has been used in traditional medicine to help maintain healthy blood sugar levels.

6

Gymnema Sylvestre

An herb that may help support healthy glucose absorption and maintain normal blood sugar levels already within the normal range.

7

Biotin

A B-vitamin that plays a role in carbohydrate metabolism and helps support overall metabolic health.

8

Vanadium

A trace mineral that may help support insulin function and glucose utilization in the body.
